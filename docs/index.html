How to read this

Confidence says how sure we are that the tracks belong to the station.

High
The order of the music records in the game files and the artists’ genre agree. For talk, show names match the station’s published line-up.
Medium
One source of evidence.
Low
Best guess.

Listening links open in a new tab. Filled opens that exact track; outlined opens a search for the title and artist, so the top result may not be the exact track. YouTube Music links are always searches.
